Plasma Effect
=============

With ``on_resize="resize"``, plasma fills the new area at its current animation
phase. Palette, frequencies and random streams are preserved. The resize
notification repaints immediately without advancing a simulation tick.
See :doc:`../resize` for the adapter contract.
